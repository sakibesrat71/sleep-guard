## Short description

Local keyword alarms for Teams and Slack notifications while you sleep.

## Full description

Rest with your mentions covered.

Sleep Guard sounds an alarm when a Microsoft Teams or Slack notification contains one of your chosen keywords during your monitoring window. Notification matching happens entirely on your Android phone.

CHOOSE WHAT WAKES YOU
Add your name, mention text, or other keywords. Matching is case-insensitive and supports partial text. Enable or disable keywords individually, and monitor Teams and Slack independently.

SET YOUR SLEEP WINDOW
Choose start and end times, including overnight schedules that cross midnight, or turn on Monitor all day. Optional conversation cooldown is separate from the built-in five-minute duplicate protection.

AN ALARM YOU CAN CHECK
Matching alerts start repeating alarm sound and vibration, with an obvious STOP ALARM button. See the available source app, sender, message, matched keyword, and received time. Open the original notification destination where available. Use Test Alarm, including a delayed test for checking your locked phone.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, backend, ads, analytics, AI, or cloud sync. Notification text is not uploaded. The app has no internet permission. Up to 100 recent alerts are kept in private device storage; clear them whenever you choose.

SETUP AND LIMITATIONS
Sleep Guard needs Notification Access and notification permission. Review battery optimisation, alarm volume, full-screen alarm access, and Do Not Disturb settings. Setup guidance includes Samsung devices; setting names vary by Android and One UI version.

Sleep Guard can inspect only information included in Android notifications. Hidden previews, muted channels, work-profile policies, disabled app notifications, and operating-system power restrictions may prevent detection. Full-screen display and background alarm playback depend on Android permissions and device settings. Test on your phone before relying on it overnight.

Sleep Guard is an independent utility and is not affiliated with Microsoft or Slack. It does not log into those services or read their message databases.
