Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Android app Sleep Guard (package dev.sleepguard), published by EN corporation. Contact: sakibesrat71@gmail.com.

What Sleep Guard does

Sleep Guard processes notification text from selected Microsoft Teams and Slack apps on your Android phone. It compares visible notification content with your chosen keywords and activates an alarm during your configured monitoring times.

Notification access and local processing

After your consent and Android Notification Access approval, the app can access notification text, titles, sender names, conversation or channel names, message timestamps, and notification destinations exposed by Android. Monitoring can occur while Sleep Guard is not open or the phone is locked. Android grants notification access broadly, but Sleep Guard filters to its supported apps that you select before extracting message text. Notifications from other apps are not processed for matching.

This information is processed only on your device. Sleep Guard does not transmit notification contents, keywords, settings, or history to EN corporation or any external server. The app has no internet permission and contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, AI, cloud synchronization, or account-login SDKs.

Information stored on your phone

Sleep Guard stores your monitoring settings and keywords, plus up to 100 recent alarm records. An alarm record can include available message text, sender, conversation, source app, matched keyword, timestamp, and dismissal status. Records remain until you clear history, they are replaced by newer records beyond the limit, you clear app storage, or you uninstall the app. Settings remain until changed, app storage is cleared, or the app is uninstalled.

Data is stored in Android private app storage. App backup is disabled, with explicit exclusions for cloud backup and device transfer. This is not a separate application-level encrypted database. Device security, backups outside the app's control, and a compromised or rooted device can affect privacy.

Lock screen and opening source apps

If you allow full-screen alarms, available message details may appear over the lock screen. The regular alarm notification omits message contents. Pressing Open App uses the source app's notification destination when available, or launches that app. That action is controlled by Android and the source application.

Your controls

You can disable Sleep Guard, select monitored apps, change or remove keywords, clear Recent Alerts, and withdraw processing consent from Setup. Withdrawn consent stops further notification processing but does not itself delete history. Revoke Notification Access through Android settings to remove the system permission. Clear history or app storage to delete saved data. There is no Sleep Guard account and no server-side account data to delete.

Permissions

Notification Access enables notification processing. Notification permission allows alarm notifications. Full-screen intent access enables the alarm interface where Android permits it. Vibration, a media-playback foreground service, and wake locks support an active alarm. Optional Notification Policy Access allows supported alarm-channel behaviour with Do Not Disturb; Sleep Guard does not silently change global DND settings or global alarm volume. The app does not request Accessibility, microphone, camera, contacts, location, overlay, or exact-alarm access.

Logs and support

Production logging does not include private notification bodies. If you voluntarily email support, your email address and the information you send are received through email and used to respond to your request. Do not send confidential Teams or Slack messages, passwords, or signing keys. Support email is separate from the app's local notification processing.

Third-party services

Teams, Slack, Google Play, Android, your email provider, and your browser operate under their own policies. This privacy-policy website is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose hosting provider may process standard web request information when you visit. Visiting this page does not send Sleep Guard's notification history to the website. Sleep Guard is not affiliated with Microsoft or Slack.

Changes and contact
